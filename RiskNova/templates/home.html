About the research

Predictive Analytics for Project Risk Management Using Machine Learning

This research focuses on identifying project risk indicators, predicting risk severity, and supporting better planning decisions using AI-assisted analytics. The system provides early warnings that help teams mitigate schedule, scope, communication, and cost-related concerns before they escalate.

Authors: Mahnoor Zaman, Nasir Umar, Amna Afzal, Afifa Saif
Affiliation: NFC Institute of Engineering and Technology, Multan, Pakistan
